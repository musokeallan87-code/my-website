Teacher Professional Development Corner

Practical ideas and resources to support effective lesson preparation and classroom teaching.

Lesson preparation

Prepare lesson objectives, teaching and learning activities, resources, assessment and reflection before teaching.

Teaching methods

Use discussion, demonstration, group work, inquiry, questioning and problem-solving to involve learners.

Classroom management

Establish clear routines, communicate expectations, encourage participation and treat learners fairly.

Assessment

Use oral questions, written exercises, projects, observation and feedback to monitor learner progress.

Suggested lesson plan checklist

  • State measurable lesson objectives.
  • Identify learners' prior knowledge.
  • Select suitable teaching methods and materials.
  • Plan activities for learner participation.
  • Prepare assessment questions.
  • Reflect on what worked and what needs improvement.
